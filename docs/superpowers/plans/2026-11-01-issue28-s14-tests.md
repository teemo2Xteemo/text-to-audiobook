## Review Focus

1. **A narrate job that also has a cache configured** (the production setup) creates no `translation` entries, even though TTS entries *are* written. The test asserts both, so a disabled cache cannot pass it.
2. **A narrate failure mid-job that is not retryable**, followed by a retry. Finished chunks are not sent to TTS again, and the final job is `COMPLETED` with one merged output.
